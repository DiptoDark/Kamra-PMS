# Bedlu PMS

Property management for small hotels, motels and guesthouses run by an owner and up to five staff. Bedlu's front-desk experience runs on top of Kamra PMS.

## Language

### Bookings

**Booking**:
A guest's commitment to stay in one room at the property for given nights.
_Avoid_: Reservation, stay (for the record itself)

**Group booking**:
Several Bookings made together for one party, usually with one payer.
_Avoid_: Multi-room booking, block

**Booking source**:
How a Booking reached the property, chosen on every Booking.
_Avoid_: Channel, origin

**Walk-in**:
A Booking source meaning the guest was standing at the desk when the Booking was made.
_Avoid_: Same-day booking, direct check-in
